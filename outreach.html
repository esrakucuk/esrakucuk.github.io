Boston University Child Development Labs

Family Recruitment Coordinator, 2022–2026

I coordinated participant recruitment across six child development laboratories and large community events in the Boston area, built partnerships with parent groups and community organizations, managed the shared participant database, and trained undergraduate research assistants in recruitment procedures.

Mentoring and inclusion

As an active member of Boston University’s Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee in Psychological and Brain Sciences, I designed and led professional development workshops for undergraduate summer research interns (2021–2022) on research readiness, careers in psychology, and graduate school applications.

Public engagement

I make developmental science accessible to the public. I create Instagram content that translates cognitive science research into practical insights for parents, educators, and students; I have given community talks on fostering flexible thinking in children; and I have developed interactive demonstrations of research tasks for public science events.
