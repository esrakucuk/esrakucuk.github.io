Teaching

I have taught psychology at Boston University and at MEF University in Istanbul, from introductory discussion sections to a course I designed myself. I like teaching that is hands-on: in Boston University’s AIM summer program, students designed their own studies, collected data, and analyzed it in SPSS. As a first-generation college student and an international scholar, I especially enjoy working with students who are finding their way into research.
